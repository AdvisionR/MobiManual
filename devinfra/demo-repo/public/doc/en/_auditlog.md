# Audit Log

The Audit Log records every change made in the console: who made it, what they
did, when, and from which IP address. Administrators and auditors can read it.

## Finding entries

Filter by user, by action, or by a date range with **From** and **To**.
**Export CSV** downloads the entries that match the filter.

## Retention

Entries are kept for 365 days and then deleted. Export the entries you need to
keep longer.
