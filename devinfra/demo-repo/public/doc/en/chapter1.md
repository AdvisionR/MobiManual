# General Information

MobiVisor is a Mobile Device Management platform. It enrols Android and iOS
devices, applies policies to them, sends them commands, and reports on their
state.

This manual describes the web console. Each console page has a corresponding
section, reachable from the **Help** link in the top bar of that page.

## Signing in

Open the console address your administrator gave you and enter your login name
and password. Accounts imported from a directory sign in with their directory
password.

Passwords for local accounts must have at least 8 characters, including at
least one letter and one digit.

After 5 failed attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes.

## Staying signed in

The console signs you out after 30 minutes without activity, and the sign-in
page then says so. Administrators can change the timeout under
**Settings > Security**.

## Language

The console is available in English, Turkish and German. Choose the language
from the menu in the top bar. The console remembers your choice, and the
**Help** link opens this manual in the same language.

## Roles

What you can see and do depends on your role: Administrator, Helpdesk or
Auditor. Pages and buttons your role cannot use are not shown. The roles are
described under Users.
