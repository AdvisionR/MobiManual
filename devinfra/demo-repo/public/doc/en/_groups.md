# Groups

Groups collect devices so that policies can be assigned once for all of them.
Every device belongs to exactly one group. A device joins the group chosen when
it was enrolled, or the **Default** group.

## Creating a group

1. Open **Groups** and select **New group**.
2. Enter a name and, optionally, a description.
3. Select **Save**.

## Moving devices to another group

Devices are moved from the Devices page: select them, choose the target group
and select **Move to group**. A moved device drops the policies of its old
group and receives those of the new one at its next check-in.

## Assigning policies

Under the group's **Policies**, choose a policy and select **Assign policy**. A
group holds at most one policy of each type: assigning a second passcode policy,
for example, replaces the first.

Policies can also be assigned from the Policies page.

## Deleting a group

Only empty groups can be deleted. Move the devices out first. The **Default**
group cannot be deleted.
