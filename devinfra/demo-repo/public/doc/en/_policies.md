# Policies

A policy is a set of settings that MobiVisor applies to every device in the
groups the policy is assigned to. There are three types: Passcode, Kiosk and
Restrictions. Each has its own page.

## Creating a policy

1. Open **Policies**.
2. Choose the type and select **New policy**.
3. Name the policy, set its settings on the page that opens, and select
   **Save**.

## Assigning a policy

Select the groups next to the policy and select **Assign to groups**. Devices
apply the policy at their next check-in.

A group holds at most one policy of each type. Assigning a second policy of the
same type to a group replaces the first.

## Compliance

A device is compliant when it has applied every policy assigned to its group.
Compliance is checked at every check-in. Non-compliant devices are marked on the
Devices page, counted on the Dashboard, and list the reasons on their Overview
tab.
